Privacy Policy

PIA (Personal Inbox App) · Last updated October 20, 2026

PIA is a desktop email app for macOS. It runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and the developer never receives your data.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to read, organize and send email in your Gmail account (the gmail.modify scope). It uses this only to show your mail, apply the actions you choose (such as archive, label, star, mark as read, move to Trash) and send the emails you write.

Where your data is stored

Email the app downloads is stored on your Mac in an encrypted local database. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except to Google, to carry out the actions you take.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The one exception is optional AI features: if you ask the app to summarize an email or draft a reply, the content of that email is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, using your own API key, to produce the result. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you request it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PIA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Signing out of an account in the app deletes its local data. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
