PIA

Personal Inbox App — a Gmail client for macOS.

PIA is a desktop email app for reading and managing Gmail accounts on a Mac. It is a personal project, not a commercial service, and is not affiliated with Google.

  • Several Gmail accounts in one unified inbox
  • Labels, stars, drafts, aliases and signatures work the same as in Gmail
  • Runs on your Mac with no servers of its own and no tracking
